DX-LR20 USB Serial Control

Direct browser control for the DX-LR20 development kit through its CH340 USB serial adapter. Your Mac identifies the adapter as WCH 1A86:7523 and the terminal device as /dev/cu.usbserial-120.

These are the actual USB-serial hardware control lines. Whether DTR/RTS are wired to RESET/BOOT on your particular DX-LR20 dev board depends on its board wiring.
